The Design Vault

An FRC Design Handbook scavenger hunt. Find the answers in the handbook to crack the secret code.

How it works

  1. Each clue tells you which handbook page and section to look in. Open the page, find the answer, and type it in.
  2. Every right answer unlocks one word of the secret code at the top of the screen.
  3. Unlock all words to open the vault. Then show your screen to your teacher.
  4. Spelling counts, but capital letters and punctuation do not. Your progress saves in this browser.
