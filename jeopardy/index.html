Click a dollar value to open a clue. Press Space to reveal the answer, then use the ✓ / ✗ buttons to score each team. T starts a 30-second timer. Esc closes a clue. Click a team name to rename it, and click a score card to give that team board control. The game saves itself in this browser, so a refresh will not lose the scores.
